I am a second-year Master’s student at the Technology and Engineering Center for Space Utilization, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I am supervised by Professor Li Ye and affiliated with the research team specializing in Computer Technology. My research focuses on the interdisciplinary field of computer vision and multimodal large language models (MLLMs), with key efforts dedicated to three aspects: optimizing the reasoning chains of multimodal models to enhance logical coherence, exploring the adaptation methods of zero-shot learning in visual tasks to improve model generalization, and advancing the practical application of related technologies in spatial information processing scenarios to support intelligent information analysis in the aerospace field.
